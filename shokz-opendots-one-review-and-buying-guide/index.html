Shokz OpenDots One: are clip-on earbuds right for you?

By Groundwork Editorial • 4 min read • Updated 2026-08-21

Key Takeaway

The Shokz OpenDots One offer a comfortable, open-ear listening experience. Learn if these clip-on earbuds suit your active lifestyle and how to find the fit.

  • What are the Shokz OpenDots One?

What are the Shokz OpenDots One?

The Shokz OpenDots One are clip-on, open-ear earbuds designed to rest on the outer ear rather than inserting into the ear canal. By using a flexible nickel-titanium plate and a soft silicone grip, these earbuds provide a secure, lightweight fit that allows you to remain aware of your surroundings while listening to audio (CNET, 2024).

Unlike traditional in-ear monitors or noise-canceling earbuds that create a physical seal, open-ear designs prioritize environmental awareness and long-term comfort. The OpenDots One utilize dual drivers to push sound toward the ear canal, a mechanism that relies on precise positioning to achieve optimal audio balance.

How does the open-ear design affect sound quality?

The sound quality of clip-on earbuds like the Shokz OpenDots One is highly dependent on how you position the device on your ear. Because these buds do not create a seal, they cannot provide the same deep bass response as traditional in-ear models, but they offer a unique, airy soundstage that keeps you connected to your environment (CNET, 2024).

According to testing, the OpenDots One stand out as some of the best-sounding clip-on buds currently available, provided the user takes the time to adjust the fit. Because the drivers sit externally, the audio profile shifts based on the angle and placement of the clip. Users can customize their listening experience further by using the official Shokz app, which allows for EQ adjustments and battery tracking.

What are the key technical specifications?

To determine if the Shokz OpenDots One meet your functional requirements, it is essential to look at their durability, battery life, and connectivity features. These earbuds are built for active use, featuring an IP54 rating, which indicates resistance to sweat and light rain, and they offer 10 hours of continuous playback on a single charge (CNET, 2024).

Key features include: 1. **Design:** A lightweight 6.5-gram build with a nickel-titanium frame that ensures the earbuds stay in place during movement. 2. **Audio:** Dolby audio support combined with dual-driver technology for a fuller sound than typical open-ear designs. 3. **Charging:** Support for wireless charging in the case, adding convenience for daily use. 4. **Versatility:** The earbuds are interchangeable, meaning they can be worn on either the left or right ear. 5. **Customization:** The Shokz mobile app provides EQ modes, firmware updates, and a "find my earbuds" feature.

Should you choose clip-on earbuds over traditional models?

Choosing between clip-on earbuds and traditional in-ear models depends on your priority: environmental awareness versus noise isolation. If you are a runner, cyclist, or someone who works in an office and needs to hear colleagues while listening to music, the open-ear design is a practical solution that prevents the "occlusion effect," where your own voice sounds muffled and boomy (SoundGuys, 2023).

If, however, your primary goal is to commute on loud public transit or focus in a noisy environment, you should opt for earbuds with active noise cancellation (ANC). Open-ear devices, by definition, allow ambient noise to enter, which can be a significant drawback in high-decibel settings. Before purchasing, assess your typical listening environment; if you value safety and long-term comfort over total silence, the OpenDots One are a high-performing choice in the open-ear category.

How to ensure the best fit for your ears

To get the most out of your clip-on earbuds, you must follow a specific process for positioning. Because they rely on the tension of the nickel-titanium plate, the fit is customizable to different ear shapes.

1. **Slide the clip:** Gently open the clip mechanism and place the driver side facing your ear canal. 2. **Adjust for pressure:** Position the clip on the cartilage of your ear so that it is secure but not pinching. 3. **Test the sound:** Play a familiar track and slightly shift the earbud up or down along the ear cartilage. You will notice the bass and treble frequencies change as the driver aligns with your canal. 4. **Secure the fit:** Once you find the "sweet spot" where the sound is balanced, ensure the silicone grip is flush against the skin to prevent the device from shifting during exercise.
