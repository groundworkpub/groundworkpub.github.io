Is Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 worth the investment?

Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 is a turn-based role-playing game that blends high-fidelity visual storytelling with mechanical depth similar to the Persona series. The game is currently priced at approximately $33.17 via Amazon for the Xbox Series X version, down from its standard $49.99 retail price. This discount represents a significant entry point for a title widely considered one of the top RPG releases of 2025.

For PC players, the game is available via third-party digital storefronts like Fanatical for $35.49. Before purchasing, confirm if you already have an active Xbox Game Pass subscription, as the title is currently included in that library, which may negate the need for a standalone purchase.

How to maximize savings on Google Pixel 11 bundles

Bundling a smartphone with a companion wearable is a common strategy to reduce the total cost of ownership for a new ecosystem. Amazon is currently offering a bundle for the Google Pixel 11 with the Pixel Watch 5 that reduces the effective cost of the watch by $130. By purchasing the base Pixel 11 (256GB) alongside the watch, the total price is $1,168 instead of the $1,298 combined retail value.

To determine if this is the right purchase for you, consider the following: 1. Evaluate your storage needs: The base 256GB model is sufficient for most users, but the Pro model offers enhanced camera arrays. 2. Compare bundle tiers: The Pixel 11 Pro bundle is priced at $1,248, which is only $100 more than the base bundle, offering a better value-to-feature ratio if you prioritize photography. 3. Verify compatibility: Ensure the included watch model meets your fitness tracking and notification requirements before committing to the bundle.

Are mid-range gaming laptops like the Gigabyte A16 a good value?

Mid-range gaming laptops are considered high-value when they feature modern GPUs and upgradable components. The Gigabyte A16, equipped with an Nvidia RTX 5070 and 32GB of DDR5 RAM, is currently available at Walmart for $1,249, a reduction of $600 from its original price.

This machine provides substantial performance for high-fidelity gaming at 1200p resolution with a 165Hz refresh rate. Key specifications to consider for long-term utility include: * GPU: The RTX 5070 provides enough headroom for most modern titles at high graphical settings. * Upgradability: The ability to expand RAM to 64GB and storage to 4TB ensures the device remains relevant as software demands increase. * Portability vs. Performance: While 16-inch laptops are less portable, they offer superior cooling and screen real estate compared to 14-inch alternatives.

Where to find the best prices on 4K Blu-ray sets

Physical media collectors can find recurring discounts on 4K Blu-ray box sets through specialized retailers like Gruv. These retailers often rotate their catalog, with current deals featuring The Matrix Deja Vu collection for $34.99 and the Jurassic World: Ultimate Collection for $49.99.

To secure the lowest price, check for "first-time shopper" codes—such as the 15% discount code often available for new accounts—which can be applied to these already discounted rates. Always compare the total cost against major retailers like Walmart or Amazon, as shipping fees and regional tax differences can impact the final price.
