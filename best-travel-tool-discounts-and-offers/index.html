How to save on essential travel tools and services

By Groundwork Editorial • 3 min read • Updated 2026-08-21

Key Takeaway

Learn how to save on essential travel tools like CLEAR+, award flight search engines, and loyalty programs to maximize your travel budget efficiently.

In This Guide

  • How to get discounts on airport security services
  • How to find the best award flight deals
  • Why you should monitor loyalty program point sales
  • How to maximize value from niche travel tools

Travel tools are software, membership services, and financial products designed to streamline airport navigation, optimize award flight bookings, and maximize loyalty program returns. Using these services can reduce physical stress at the airport and lower the effective cost of your travel by helping you secure better redemption rates for points and miles.

According to industry analysis, travelers who utilize automated award-searching tools can identify flight redemptions that provide 20% to 50% more value per point compared to manual searches. By leveraging discounted access to these platforms, you can recoup the cost of the subscription within a single international booking.

How to get discounts on airport security services

Airport security services like CLEAR+ are identity verification platforms that allow you to bypass standard document checks, significantly reducing wait times. A standard annual membership for CLEAR+ typically costs $219 per year, but you can often reduce this through strategic partnerships and promotional offers that provide first-year discounts ranging from $40 to $80.

To maximize your savings, check if your premium travel credit card offers a statement credit for CLEAR+ memberships. Many high-end American Express and Chase cards provide a full annual credit that covers the cost of the membership entirely. You can stack these institutional benefits with public promotional codes to effectively eliminate out-of-pocket expenses while maintaining your status as a member.

How to find the best award flight deals

Award flight search engines, such as Point.me, are platforms that aggregate flight inventory across multiple airline loyalty programs to show you exactly how many points you need for a seat. These tools are essential for travelers who want to avoid the time-consuming process of searching individual airline websites.

1. Identify your desired destination and travel dates. 2. Use an award search tool to compare point requirements across different airline alliances. 3. Apply promotional codes or seasonal discounts to annual plans to lower your subscription cost. 4. Book your flight directly through the airline once the tool identifies the lowest point redemption rate.

By securing a 12-month plan at a discounted rate, you lower your monthly cost of access, making it more feasible to use the tool for smaller, frequent domestic trips rather than just expensive international ones.

Why you should monitor loyalty program point sales

Loyalty program point sales occur when airlines or hotel chains offer "buy points" promotions, often featuring a 100% to 150% bonus on purchased points. A point sale is a strategy where you purchase points at a price lower than the average value you would receive when redeeming them for premium flights or hotel stays.

Before purchasing points during a sale, calculate the cost per point. If the purchase price is lower than the typical redemption value—often measured in cents per point—you have created an arbitrage opportunity. Always ensure you have a specific redemption in mind before buying, as points are non-refundable and subject to devaluation by the loyalty program provider.

How to maximize value from niche travel tools

Niche travel tools, including ExpertFlyer and Points Path, provide granular data on flight availability, upgrade inventory, and point-to-cash value comparisons. These services are most effective for advanced travelers who regularly monitor flight "alerts" for last-minute seat openings or specific fare classes.

If you are a frequent traveler, consider subscribing to newsletters that aggregate these niche offers. Often, the most significant discounts for these specific tools are hidden behind subscriber-only links or private promotional codes. By automating your access to these tools, you ensure you are always monitoring the market for the best possible travel value without manual intervention.
