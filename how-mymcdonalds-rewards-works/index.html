How to maximize the MyMcDonald's Rewards program

By Groundwork Editorial • 4 min read • Updated 2026-08-21

Key Takeaway

Learn how MyMcDonald's Rewards works, how to earn 100 points per dollar, and strategies to maximize your savings through point redemptions and in-app deals.

In This Guide

  • How to earn points at McDonald's
  • Understanding the redemption tiers
  • How to stack rewards and deals
  • Limitations and best practices

MyMcDonald's Rewards is a loyalty program that allows customers to earn points on every dollar spent at participating U.S. McDonald’s locations, which can then be redeemed for free menu items. Unlike complex travel loyalty programs, this system operates on a straightforward earn-and-burn model, providing immediate value for frequent diners through point-based redemptions and exclusive in-app offers.

According to the official McDonald's rewards structure, members earn 100 points for every $1 spent on eligible purchases (Source: McDonald's). While the program underwent a devaluation in 2023 that increased the point requirements for many items by 500 points, it remains a consistent way to offset the cost of fast-food purchases for regular customers.

How to earn points at McDonald's

Earning points requires active participation via the McDonald’s mobile app. There are three primary ways to accumulate your balance:

1. Mobile ordering: Place your order directly through the app for pickup or delivery. Points are automatically credited to your account upon purchase. 2. Drive-thru or counter: Before paying at the register or the drive-thru window, you must scan your unique four-digit code found in the 'Rewards & Deals' section of the app. 3. McDelivery: If you order through the app using the delivery feature, your points are tracked automatically, provided you are logged into your account.

It is important to note that points do not accrue automatically based on your payment card. You must proactively scan your code or order through the app to ensure your transaction is recorded.

Understanding the redemption tiers

Redemption is structured into fixed point tiers, meaning the cost of an item in points remains constant regardless of the local menu price. This can create opportunities for higher value depending on your location.

* 1,500 points: This is the entry-level tier, typically covering smaller items like a McChicken, cheeseburger, or a small order of fries. * 3,000 points: This tier usually includes mid-range items such as a medium fry, large soda, or a McDouble. * 4,500 points: This tier often covers more substantial items, including a Big Mac, Quarter Pounder with Cheese, or a 10-piece Chicken McNuggets. * 6,000 points: The highest tier typically reserved for premium menu items or larger meal components.

Because prices for these items vary significantly by city and state, the effective 'value' of your points changes based on where you redeem them. If you live in an area with higher menu prices, your points effectively go further compared to locations where those items are cheaper (Source: The Points Guy).

How to stack rewards and deals

Beyond point accumulation, the most effective way to save money is by stacking rewards with the 'Deals' section of the app. McDonald's frequently offers limited-time promotions, such as 'Free medium fries with any $1 purchase' or 'Buy one, get one' offers.

These deals are often compatible with point earning. For instance, if you apply a deal for a free item with a minimum purchase, you will still earn points on the dollar amount spent to trigger that deal. By checking the app before every visit, you can ensure that you are not missing out on promotional savings that exist outside of the standard point-redemption ecosystem.

Limitations and best practices

While the program is simple, there are specific constraints to keep in mind:

* Point expiration: Points typically expire on the first day of the month after the six-month anniversary of when they were earned. Monitor your balance in the app to avoid losing accumulated points. * Menu availability: Not all items are available for redemption at all times. Breakfast items, for example, may not be eligible for redemption during lunch or dinner hours. * No elite status: Unlike airline or hotel loyalty programs, there are no tiers or status benefits. Your rewards are strictly limited to the points you earn and the deals available to all users.

To get the most out of the program, prioritize using your points on items that have the highest local menu price, rather than just the items you prefer. By treating the app as a tool to capture both promotional discounts and base points, you can maximize your return on every dollar spent.
