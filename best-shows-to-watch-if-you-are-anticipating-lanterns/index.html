Best shows to watch if you are anticipating lanterns

By Elena Vance • 4 min read • Updated 2026-08-21

Key Takeaway

Prepare for the HBO series Lanterns by watching these grounded, investigative, and character-driven shows that redefine the superhero genre.

  • What makes a great grounded superhero show?

  • 2. Green Lantern: The Animated Series (2011–2013)
  • 3. True Detective (Season 1)
  • 4. Legion (2017–2019)
  • How to prepare for the premiere of lanterns

A television show that blends grounded, small-town mystery with high-stakes superhuman conflict is a rare feat in modern genre storytelling. As DC Studios prepares to launch *Lanterns*, an upcoming HBO series starring Kyle Chandler and Aaron Pierre, audiences are being introduced to a narrative that moves away from traditional galactic space-cop tropes. Instead, the show centers on a murder investigation in rural Nebraska, echoing the 'hard-boiled' investigative tone often found in neo-noir crime dramas.

According to James Gunn’s DC Universe roadmap, *Lanterns* is intended to redefine how superhero properties interact with human environments (Variety, 2024). By examining the intersection of cosmic power and localized human struggle, the series draws inspiration from legacy comic arcs where characters like Hal Jordan moved from intergalactic patrols to boots-on-the-ground social commentary.

What makes a great grounded superhero show?

A grounded superhero show is a narrative that prioritizes character-driven mystery, political subtext, and human vulnerability over spectacle-based CGI battles. These series succeed by treating their superhuman elements as catalysts for exploring societal themes rather than as the primary focus of the plot. By limiting the scope of power, these shows force protagonists to rely on intellect and morality, making the stakes feel personal rather than existential.

1. Watchmen (2019)

*Watchmen* is a prestige limited series that serves as a sequel to the seminal 1986 graphic novel, exploring the intersection of historical trauma and vigilante justice in an alternate Tulsa, Oklahoma. It is arguably the gold standard for blending grounded police procedural elements with the deconstruction of superhero tropes. Like *Lanterns*, it explores how individuals with immense power—or those policing them—navigate the complexities of real-world history and systemic oppression. The show utilizes a methodical, mystery-first approach, ensuring the audience is as invested in the forensic investigation as they are in the masked protagonists.

2. Green Lantern: The Animated Series (2011–2013)

For viewers who want to understand the core lore of the Green Lantern Corps before the HBO series premieres, *Green Lantern: The Animated Series* provides the most faithful adaptation of the character dynamics. While this show leans heavily into space opera rather than terrestrial mystery, it excels at defining the internal tensions within the Corps. The show explores the morality of the Guardians of the Universe and the psychological toll of wielding a power ring, providing essential context for the veteran status of characters like Hal Jordan.

3. True Detective (Season 1)

While not a superhero show, *True Detective* is the atmospheric blueprint for any series involving a "buddy-cop" dynamic investigating a mystery in a rural setting. Its influence on *Lanterns* is evident, as the upcoming DC series is explicitly modeled after the investigative tension found in gritty, character-heavy crime dramas. Watching this series will prepare you for the specific pacing and tone of a high-stakes investigation set against the backdrop of a small, isolated town.

4. Legion (2017–2019)

*Legion* is a psychological thriller that challenges the viewer's perception of reality, focusing on David Haller, a man diagnosed with schizophrenia who discovers his visions are actually manifestations of immense telepathic power. This series is essential viewing for those interested in how superhero shows can experiment with narrative structure and unreliable narrators. It mirrors the experimental spirit that *Lanterns* aims to bring to the DC Universe.

How to prepare for the premiere of lanterns

To best prepare for the series, you should focus on viewing titles that prioritize slow-burn mystery over rapid-fire action sequences.

1. Identify the specific tone of the show by watching the *Watchmen* limited series to understand how DC properties can tackle serious, real-world themes. 2. Review the core character history of Hal Jordan and John Stewart through the 2011 animated series to understand the emotional weight of their partnership. 3. Familiarize yourself with the neo-noir genre by watching modern investigative thrillers, which will help you appreciate the shift toward a "space cop" mystery format.

By engaging with these shows, you gain a better understanding of how the genre is evolving from spectacle-heavy blockbusters to character-focused, grounded storytelling.
