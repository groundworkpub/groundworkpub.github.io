Avios is the rewards currency used by 7 airline loyalty programs, including Aer Lingus AerClub, British Airways Club, Finnair Plus, Iberia Club, Loganair Loyalty, Qatar Airways Privilege Club, and Vueling Club. You can transfer Avios between any of these programs at a ratio of 1:1 in any direction at no cost as often as you like, with generous annual limits. A key data point to consider is that each Avios program prices award redemptions slightly differently, so it's essential to have your Avios in the right program to take advantage of sweet spots in each, as noted by The Points Guy. ## what is avios and how does it work Avios is a rewards currency that can be earned and redeemed with any of the 7 airline loyalty programs. According to the Avios website, you can earn Avios through flights, credit card spending, and other partner activities. You can also transfer Avios between programs to maximize your rewards. ## how to transfer avios between accounts To transfer Avios, you need to have a loyalty account with each program you want to transfer to, as stated on the Avios website. Your accounts must have been open for at least 30 days before you can transfer Avios. Here's how to transfer Avios: 1. Ensure you have created a loyalty account with each program you want to transfer Avios to. 2. Log in to the Avios website with any of your affiliated loyalty program account credentials (excluding Qatar Airways Privilege Club). 3. Select the program you want to transfer Avios from and to. 4. Complete the two-factor authentication when prompted and wait for the confirmation message. ## how to link your avios accounts To link your Avios accounts, follow these steps: 1. Log in to the account you wish to link. 2. Pass the two-factor authentication. 3. Confirm the link (once established, the linked accounts are permanent). ## how to earn avios You can earn Avios through various methods, including: 1. Having an Avios-earning credit card. 2. Purchasing Avios. 3. Earning rewards with retail partners. 4. Transferring Avios from a credit card program, as noted by The Points Guy. In terms of earning transferable rewards, NerdWallet suggests that transferring Avios from a credit card program can be a convenient option for many travelers. It's essential to note that while Avios transfers are generally instant, there may be some exceptions. As stated on the Avios website, transfers to and from Qatar Airways Privilege Club must go via British Airways Club. ## tips and considerations When transferring Avios, consider the following tips and considerations: 1. Ensure you use the same name, email address, and date of birth for each account so they can be linked. 2. There is no minimum number of Avios to transfer, but most members are limited to transfers of 999,999 Avios per transaction and 1,000,000 Avios per rolling 12-month period. 3. Be aware of the sweet spots in each program to maximize your rewards, as noted by The Points Guy. By following these steps and considering these tips, you can easily transfer Avios between airline loyalty programs and maximize your rewards. Your next step is to log in to the Avios website and start transferring your Avios to the program that offers the best value for your needs. You can also consider earning Avios through credit cards, retail partners, or other methods to increase your rewards balance. Remember to always check the terms and conditions of each program before transferring Avios, as they may change over time. By staying informed and taking advantage of the Avios transfer tool, you can make the most of your airline loyalty program rewards.
