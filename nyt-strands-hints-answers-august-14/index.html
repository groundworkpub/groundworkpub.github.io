How to solve the NYT Strands puzzle for August 14

By Alex Rivera • 3 min read • Updated 2026-08-21

Key Takeaway

Struggling with the August 14 NYT Strands puzzle? Get the daily theme, spangram, and all answers for puzzle #894 to help you finish your game.

A NYT Strands puzzle is a daily word search game where players must identify words related to a specific theme hidden within a grid of letters. Unlike traditional word searches, Strands requires you to find a 'spangram'—a theme-related word that spans the entire board from one side to the other—while ensuring every letter in the grid is used exactly once to complete the puzzle.

What is the theme for the August 14 puzzle?

The theme for the August 14 NYT Strands puzzle (number 894) is 'Discovered by accident.' This theme refers to the history of scientific and product development where common items were created unintentionally rather than through a deliberate design process. If you are struggling to find the connection, the puzzle focuses on famous accidental inventions that have become household staples.

How do you find the hidden words?

To find the hidden words in a Strands puzzle, you must scan the grid for sequences of letters that form valid English words of four letters or more. When you successfully find three words of four letters or more, the game automatically reveals one of the theme-related words as a hint. If you are stuck, look for common letter combinations first, as these often lead to longer words that help clear the board.

For the August 14 puzzle, common words that can help you unlock hints include: PALS, SLAP, SLAT, PROW, RAVE, PAVE, CAVE, WAVE, COIL, COILS, COIN, COINS, WOOL, PENT, CENT, and LINK. By finding these, you gain the necessary progress to reveal the theme-specific answers without guessing randomly.

What are the answers for the August 14 Strands?

The theme-related answers for this puzzle revolve around items that were famously discovered or invented by chance. Identifying these words requires connecting the letters on the board in a continuous path. The answers for this specific puzzle are:

1. SLINKY 2. VELCRO 3. PLASTIC 4. MICROWAVE 5. PENICILLIN

Each of these items has a documented history of accidental discovery. For example, the microwave was invented after an engineer noticed a candy bar melted in his pocket while standing near a magnetron, and Penicillin was famously discovered by Alexander Fleming when he noticed mold killing bacteria in an unwashed petri dish.

How do you identify the spangram?

The spangram is the most critical part of a Strands puzzle, as it connects the two sides of the board and provides the primary context for the theme. For the August 14 puzzle, the spangram is 'INVENTIONS.' To locate it, look for the 'I' on the top row and trace the path downward across the grid. Finding the spangram early is highly recommended because it effectively splits the remaining letters into two manageable sections, making it easier to find the remaining theme words.

Why are some Strands puzzles harder than others?

Strands puzzles vary in difficulty based on the obscurity of the theme and the complexity of the letter arrangement. Puzzles focusing on niche topics like 'dated slang' or 'marine biology' often prove more difficult because they require specific vocabulary knowledge. If you find yourself frequently stuck, prioritize finding the spangram first. Research into game design patterns suggests that once the spangram is identified, the remaining words often become significantly easier to decipher because the theme's scope is narrowed. Always focus on connecting letters that appear to form patterns rather than searching for individual words in isolation.
