How to earn rewards with the Chase Refer-A-Friend program

A Chase referral bonus is a loyalty incentive that rewards you with points, miles, or cash back when someone you refer is approved for a new Chase credit card through your unique link. This program allows you to earn supplemental rewards without the need for additional personal spending, provided the person you refer meets the specific application criteria.

According to data from Chase’s program terms, cardholders can earn between 10,000 and 40,000 points or miles per successful referral, depending on the specific card product and current promotional offers.

How the Chase referral process works

The referral process is designed to be straightforward, requiring you to generate a unique digital link that tracks the application back to your account. You do not need to contact customer service to initiate this; the entire process is automated through Chase’s secure digital portals.

1. Log in to your Chase account via the mobile app or the official Chase Refer-A-Friend website. 2. Navigate to the referral dashboard, which lists all of your eligible credit cards. 3. Select the specific card you wish to refer. The system will generate a unique URL. 4. Share this link directly with your friend, family member, or colleague. 5. Ensure your referral uses that specific link to submit their application.

You will only receive the bonus if the applicant is approved after using your link. If the applicant navigates away or uses a different link, the system cannot attribute the referral to your account.

Understanding referral bonus limits

While the program is a reliable way to boost your rewards balance, Chase enforces strict annual caps on how much you can earn through referrals. These limits are designed to prevent excessive use of the program and ensure compliance with regulatory standards regarding credit card marketing.

Most Chase cardholders are subject to a calendar-year cap ranging from 50,000 to 200,000 bonus points or miles. For cash-back products, such as the Chase Freedom series, there is typically a maximum annual payout of $500 per card account. It is vital to check your specific referral dashboard, as these caps reset annually on January 1st. Once you hit your limit for a specific card, you will stop receiving bonuses for that calendar year, even if you continue to refer new applicants.

Eligibility and card participation

Not every Chase credit card is eligible for the Refer-A-Friend program at all times. Chase periodically updates its list of participating cards based on marketing goals and inventory. Generally, the program covers a wide range of products, including:

* Cobranded airline cards (e.g., United, Southwest) * Hotel loyalty cards (e.g., Marriott Bonvoy, IHG, World of Hyatt) * Chase Sapphire series (Preferred and Reserve) * Chase Freedom series (Unlimited and Flex) * Select business and personal checking accounts

If you do not see a specific card in your referral dashboard, it is likely that the card is not currently participating in the program. You should check back periodically, as eligibility can change based on current promotional cycles.

Best practices for sharing your link

To maximize your success, share your link only with people who are genuinely interested in the card’s benefits. The person you refer must be approved for the card for you to earn your bonus. If you are sharing the link on social media or professional networks, ensure you disclose that you are a cardholder and may receive a benefit from their application. This transparency is not only good practice but is often required by platform guidelines and consumer protection laws. Avoid spamming or using high-pressure tactics, as these are rarely effective and can result in your account being flagged for violating terms of service.
