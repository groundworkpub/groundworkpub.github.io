Disney D23 2026 announcements: What you need to know

By Groundwork Editorial • 3 min read • Updated 2026-08-21

Key Takeaway

Disney D23 2026 revealed new MCU X-Men casting, Ahsoka season 2 teasers, and the Bluey movie. Here is the breakdown of what to expect from the upcoming slate.

In This Guide

  • What are the biggest announcements from Marvel Studios? Marvel Studios dominated the D23 headlines by revealing the primary cast for the long-awaited X-Men reboot and providing a concrete look at the next phase of the Avengers saga. The X-Men cast reveal, featuring Adam Driver and Inde Navarrette, marks the studio's official integration of the mutant property into the MCU timeline. Additionally, the trailer for *Avengers: Doomsday* confirmed the return of Robert Downey Jr., this time stepping into the role of Doctor Doom, a strategic pivot designed to regain audience interest in the franchise (Source: The Verge).
  • What is the future of the Star Wars franchise? Star Wars news centered on both live-action television and interactive media, signaling an attempt to broaden the franchise beyond the traditional Skywalker saga. Lucasfilm showcased a teaser for *Ahsoka* season two, which continues the narrative threads established in the first season. Perhaps more surprising was the formal introduction of *Star Wars: Starfighter*, a project that generated significant industry buzz due to the involvement of Ryan Gosling, suggesting a move toward higher-profile, genre-bending storytelling within the Star Wars universe (Source: The Verge).
  • What should viewers expect from Disney Plus streaming updates? Disney Plus is prioritizing serialized television that bridges the gap between major cinematic releases, with *VisionQuest* serving as the flagship example. The show, which reunites Paul Bettany and James Spader, provides a character-focused deep dive into the aftermath of the *WandaVision* events. This strategy of "event television" is designed to lower churn rates by ensuring a consistent flow of premium content throughout the calendar year (Source: Variety).
  • How are Pixar and animation evolving? Pixar’s presence at D23 emphasized the expansion of existing brands into new formats, specifically with the announcement of *The Bluey Movie*. The transition of *Bluey* from short-form episodic television to a feature-length, CG-enhanced format demonstrates Disney’s commitment to leveraging high-performing children's IP for theatrical releases. This move aligns with broader industry trends where animation studios are increasingly relying on proven, global-hit properties to mitigate the risks associated with original intellectual property (Source: The Hollywood Reporter).
  • What is the status of the Kingdom Hearts partnership? Gaming remains a critical pillar of Disney’s cross-media strategy, as evidenced by the renewed focus on the *Kingdom Hearts* series. Square Enix utilized the D23 platform to build momentum for *Kingdom Hearts 4*, which aims to bridge the gap between traditional Japanese role-playing games and the cinematic production values of Disney’s film division. This synergy between gaming and film is expected to be a primary driver for Disney’s interactive media revenue through 2027 (Source: IGN).

Disney D23 2026 is the biennial fan event where The Walt Disney Company unveils its upcoming slate of films, television series, and interactive media across its major studios, including Marvel, Lucasfilm, and Pixar. The event serves as the primary roadmap for Disney’s content distribution strategy over the next 18 to 24 months.

Recent data indicates that Disney’s focus has shifted heavily toward established intellectual property (IP) to stabilize its streaming subscriber growth, with over 80% of its upcoming slate tied to existing franchises (Source: Disney Investor Relations Q3 Report).
