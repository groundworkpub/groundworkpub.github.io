A guide to the renovated The Wing, First lounge at Hong Kong International

The Wing, First is a premium airport lounge located at Hong Kong International Airport (HKG) that serves as a flagship space for Cathay Pacific’s first-class passengers and top-tier loyalty members. Following an 11-month renovation that concluded in April 2024, the lounge maintains its signature residential aesthetic while refining its dining, service, and wellness offerings to create a more streamlined travel experience.

According to Cathay Pacific, the lounge is situated near gates 1 through 4 in Terminal 1, making it a convenient stop for travelers departing from the northern end of the airport. While the facility is designed to offer a tranquil environment, its location requires passengers departing from higher-numbered gates to account for additional transit time to their boarding area.

Understanding lounge access requirements

Access to The Wing, First is currently restricted to a specific subset of travelers as part of the airline’s "initial reopening period." Cathay Pacific notes that entry is limited to passengers booked in Cathay Pacific first class and members holding Diamond status within the Cathay loyalty program (Cathay Pacific, 2024).

This policy represents a temporary change for Oneworld Emerald members who are not flying in Cathay Pacific first class or do not hold Cathay Diamond status. These travelers are currently redirected to other facilities, such as The Pier, First, or the airline’s various business-class lounges like The Bridge. While the airline has not provided a specific date for when broader Oneworld Emerald access will be restored, it has explicitly stated that the current restrictions are not permanent (The Points Guy, 2024).

Dining options and culinary variety

Dining at The Wing, First is structured to accommodate both travelers seeking a leisurely, multi-course meal and those looking for a quick, high-quality bite. The lounge features three primary service areas:

1. The Dining Room: This space offers a formal, sit-down experience with a full menu. It frequently hosts rotating specials, including collaborative menus from high-end restaurants like Mott 32, as well as dedicated wellness-focused selections. 2. The Atrium: Designed as a lighter, more casual environment, the Atrium provides an a la carte menu that is ideal for shorter stays or travelers who prefer a brighter atmosphere. 3. The Pantry: This self-service area is intended for rapid access to snacks, hot dishes, and refreshments, catering to passengers who do not have time for formal table service.

Travelers are encouraged to utilize these distinct zones based on their schedule. For instance, it is common practice to visit the Dining Room for a signature dish, such as dan dan noodles, before moving to the Atrium for a lighter snack or beverage. Champagne service is also available via a mobile cart located near the entrance, providing a high-touch service element that characterizes the lounge’s elevated status.

Wellness and residential design

The renovation maintains the "residential" design philosophy that has defined Cathay Pacific’s lounge brand for years. By utilizing warm woods, natural light, and spacious seating arrangements, the lounge aims to mitigate the stress of air travel. A core component of this experience is the wellness area, which features the lounge's iconic cabanas. These private spaces are highly sought after, offering travelers a quiet, enclosed environment to rest, shower, or work in solitude before their flight.

How to maximize your visit

To make the most of your time at The Wing, First, follow these steps:

1. Verify your eligibility: Before heading to gates 1-4, confirm that your current ticket or status level meets the temporary reopening criteria to avoid being redirected to another lounge. 2. Monitor your boarding time: Because the lounge is located at the start of Terminal 1, ensure you check the distance to your specific gate. If you are departing from the 40s or 60s, allocate at least 15-20 minutes for transit through the terminal. 3. Plan your dining strategy: Review the menus in the Dining Room versus the Atrium early in your visit. If you want a specific collaboration dish, head to the Dining Room first, as these options may have limited availability. 4. Secure a cabana: If you intend to use the wellness facilities, inquire about availability with the reception desk immediately upon arrival to secure a time slot, as these are often in high demand.
