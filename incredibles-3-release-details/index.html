The Incredibles 3 is an upcoming computer-animated superhero film produced by Pixar Animation Studios that continues the story of the Parr family. Confirmed by Pixar Chief Creative Officer Pete Docter during the 2024 D23 Entertainment Showcase, the project marks the third installment in the franchise, which first premiered in 2004 (CNET, 2024).

According to data from Box Office Mojo, the original *The Incredibles* grossed over $631 million worldwide, while its 2018 sequel, *Incredibles 2*, became the highest-grossing animated film of its time, surpassing $1.2 billion globally. This financial track record establishes the franchise as one of Pixar's most reliable intellectual properties.

What is the plot of The Incredibles 3?

The Incredibles 3 will focus on the maturation of the Parr children—Violet, Dash, and Jack-Jack—as they navigate their roles within a world where superheroes are once again legal. Pete Docter confirmed that the narrative centers on the siblings' desire for independence, with the characters actively sneaking out at night to pursue their own agendas (CNET, 2024).

As the family struggles to maintain control over the youngest child, Jack-Jack, they will also face a new supervillain. The film is expected to explore the dynamics of the "Super Hero League," an organization established in the wake of the second film's conclusion, which effectively legalized superhero activity. This shift from the family-only focus of the first two films suggests a broader world-building approach, integrating the Parrs into a formal infrastructure of heroes.

Who is involved in the production?

While Pixar has confirmed the project is in active development, specific details regarding the full voice cast and directorial team remain limited. Brad Bird, who wrote and directed the first two films, has been the creative anchor for the series; however, Pixar has not yet officially confirmed his return for the third installment. The studio is currently focusing on the 40th anniversary of Pixar, using this milestone to pivot toward legacy sequels and new original content.

Production at Pixar currently follows a rigorous development cycle. Given the complexity of animation, the studio typically spends four to five years on a high-budget feature, which aligns with the announced timeline for this release. The studio is balancing this project alongside other major franchise sequels, including *Frozen 3* and *Toy Story 5*.

When will The Incredibles 3 be released?

The Incredibles 3 is scheduled for a theatrical release in the summer of 2028 (CNET, 2024). This timeline provides Pixar with a substantial window to refine the script and visual effects, ensuring the film meets the high technical standards expected of the studio's flagship sequels.

If you want to prepare for the upcoming release, you can watch the first two films on Disney Plus. Streaming these entries provides necessary context for the status of the Super Hero League and the evolution of the Parr children's powers, which serve as the foundation for the upcoming sequel.

To stay informed about official updates, monitor the Disney D23 fan club newsletters and the official Pixar social media channels. These platforms are the primary sources for casting announcements, teaser trailers, and finalized release dates. Avoid relying on unofficial social media speculation, as major plot details and release shifts are only confirmed through official press releases from The Walt Disney Company.
