Understanding the American Express Fine Hotels & Resorts program expansion

By Marcus Vance • 5 min read • Updated 2026-08-21

Key Takeaway

The American Express Fine Hotels & Resorts program has expanded to over 1,500 properties. Learn how to leverage these perks to maximize your travel value.

Outline

  • How the Fine Hotels & Resorts program works
  • The impact of the recent expansion
  • Evaluating the cost-to-benefit ratio
  • Strategic use of the annual $200 hotel credit
  • Considerations for frequent travelers

The American Express Fine Hotels & Resorts (FHR) program is a curated collection of luxury properties that provides specific, guaranteed perks to cardholders of The Platinum Card from American Express and The Business Platinum Card. With the recent addition of 351 properties to the portfolio, the program now encompasses over 1,500 hotels globally, offering travelers standardized benefits that bypass the need for traditional hotel loyalty elite status (American Express, 2024). These benefits are designed to add tangible value to luxury stays, including room upgrades, late check-out, and daily breakfast credits.

How the Fine Hotels & Resorts program works

The FHR program functions as a premium travel booking portal for specific American Express cardholders. When you book a stay through the American Express Travel website, you are entitled to a suite of benefits at every participating property. Unlike standard hotel bookings, which may vary by brand or status level, FHR guarantees the following perks at every location:

1. Noon check-in, when available. 2. Room upgrade upon arrival, when available. 3. Daily breakfast for two people. 4. A property-specific amenity, such as a $100 food and beverage or spa credit. 5. Guaranteed 4:00 p.m. late check-out. 6. Complimentary Wi-Fi.

According to data from American Express, these benefits represent an average total value of $550 per stay, though actual savings depend on the specific property's pricing and the individual's travel habits (American Express, 2024). Because these benefits are attached to the card rather than the hotel's loyalty program, you can access luxury treatment even if you do not hold high-tier status with brands like Marriott, Hilton, or Hyatt.

The impact of the recent expansion

The addition of 351 new properties represents a significant effort by American Express to broaden the geographic reach of its premium travel portfolio. Previously, the FHR collection was heavily concentrated in major financial hubs and primary tourist destinations. The expansion includes a wider array of boutique hotels and properties in secondary markets, which provides Platinum cardholders more flexibility when planning vacations in less traditional locations (NerdWallet, 2024).

This expansion is part of a broader trend in the credit card industry to prioritize travel benefits that offer immediate, measurable value. By increasing the number of participating hotels, American Express aims to ensure that the $695 annual fee associated with the Platinum card remains justifiable for frequent travelers who may struggle to find participating properties in their preferred destinations. While the sheer volume of hotels has increased, the core value proposition remains the consistency of the experience across the entire network.

Evaluating the cost-to-benefit ratio

While the FHR program provides significant perks, it is essential to evaluate whether the booking price is competitive. You must book your stay through the Amex Travel portal to receive these benefits, which means you are essentially using a third-party booking agency. In some cases, the nightly rates listed on the Amex Travel site may be higher than the 'member rate' found directly on the hotel's website.

To determine if an FHR booking makes financial sense, follow these steps:

1. Check the nightly rate on the hotel’s direct website and compare it to the rate listed on the Amex Travel portal. 2. Calculate the value of the FHR benefits, specifically the breakfast credit and the $100 property credit, against any price discrepancy. 3. Verify if the hotel is part of a loyalty program where you would earn points or elite night credits; note that FHR bookings are often treated as 'eligible' for loyalty points, but this can vary by chain. 4. Determine if the 4:00 p.m. late check-out is useful for your specific flight schedule.

If the difference in price is less than the value of the breakfast and property credits, the FHR booking is typically the superior financial choice. However, if the portal price is significantly inflated, the benefits may be effectively negated (The Points Guy, 2024).

Strategic use of the annual $200 hotel credit

One of the most effective ways to utilize the FHR program is by pairing it with the annual $200 hotel statement credit provided to Platinum cardholders. This credit applies specifically to prepaid bookings made through the Fine Hotels & Resorts or The Hotel Collection programs. Because the credit is an annual benefit, using it effectively can reduce the out-of-pocket cost of a luxury stay significantly.

To maximize this credit, you should plan at least one trip annually that utilizes an FHR-eligible property. By combining the $200 statement credit with the $100 property credit provided at check-in, you can effectively reduce the cost of a one-night stay by $300. This strategy turns a high-end luxury property into a more accessible option for weekend getaways or quick business trips.

Considerations for frequent travelers

While the program is robust, it is not a one-size-fits-all solution. If you are a loyalist to a specific hotel brand, you may find more value in pursuing elite status directly through that brand's loyalty program. Elite status often provides benefits such as lounge access, suite upgrades, and point multipliers that FHR does not cover. Furthermore, FHR benefits are limited to properties within the curated list; if you are staying in a budget property or a chain outside of the luxury tier, these perks will not apply.

Ultimately, the expansion to 1,500+ properties makes the FHR program a more versatile tool for the average Platinum cardholder. By focusing on the tangible value of the breakfast and property credits, you can ensure that your travel spend is optimized, regardless of where your destination may be.
