What to know about the new rivian r1s captain's chairs

By Groundwork Editorial • 4 min read • Updated 2026-08-21

Key Takeaway

Rivian is adding second-row captain's chairs to the R1S. Learn how this configuration improves third-row access and what it means for your vehicle order.

In This Guide

  • Why are captain's chairs a significant upgrade for the R1S?
  • Can current R1S owners retrofit their vehicles?
  • How do captain's chairs impact total vehicle capacity?
  • What are the next steps for prospective buyers?

A captain’s chair configuration is a seating layout for SUVs that replaces a standard second-row bench seat with two individual, standalone seats separated by a center aisle. Rivian has officially announced that this long-requested feature will be available for its R1S three-row electric SUV, addressing a primary pain point for families and frequent passengers.

For years, the absence of a center pass-through in the R1S has been a frequent subject of feedback from owners (The Verge, 2024). While the bench seat provides an eighth seat for potential occupants, the lack of an aisle makes accessing the third row difficult, especially when child safety seats are installed in the second row. By introducing captain's chairs, Rivian is aligning the R1S with market competitors like the Kia EV9 and the Tesla Model X, which have long offered this configuration to improve cabin ergonomics.

Why are captain's chairs a significant upgrade for the R1S?

Captain's chairs improve the R1S by providing a clear center aisle, which allows for easier entry and exit to the third row without needing to slide or tilt the second-row seats forward. This configuration is particularly beneficial for parents who frequently use child safety seats, as the aisle provides a dedicated space to manage children or access items in the rear of the vehicle without disrupting the seat setup.

Beyond accessibility, individual chairs often offer a more premium feel, providing better lateral support and individual armrests. In many luxury SUVs, this layout is preferred for its ability to create a sense of personal space for second-row passengers. According to industry data on consumer preferences for SUVs, vehicles offering multiple seating configurations tend to retain higher value and appeal to a broader range of demographics, specifically those prioritizing family utility (Automotive News, 2023).

Can current R1S owners retrofit their vehicles?

Currently, Rivian has not indicated that it will offer a retrofit program for existing R1S owners to swap their bench seats for captain's chairs. Because the seating hardware is integrated into the vehicle's floor mounting points, safety sensors, and restraint systems, swapping these components is a complex engineering task that goes beyond simple bolt-on modifications.

If you own an existing R1S, you are likely limited to the current bench seat configuration for the foreseeable future. Attempting to install aftermarket seating solutions carries significant safety risks, as these seats must be crash-tested to comply with federal motor vehicle safety standards. Modifying the interior in this way could also void your vehicle's warranty and compromise the integrity of the airbag deployment zones (NHTSA, 2024).

How do captain's chairs impact total vehicle capacity?

Choosing captain's chairs reduces the total passenger capacity of the R1S from eight occupants to seven. While losing one seat might seem like a disadvantage, for most families, the trade-off of increased utility and easier third-row access outweighs the loss of an extra center seat, which is often too cramped for adult comfort anyway.

When deciding on your configuration, consider your daily usage patterns. If you frequently transport seven or more people, the bench seat remains the more practical choice. However, if your primary goal is maximizing comfort for four or five passengers and ensuring that third-row access is effortless, the captain's chairs will provide a superior user experience. This shift reflects a broader trend in the EV market where manufacturers are prioritizing "living room" cabin layouts over maximum seat counts.

What are the next steps for prospective buyers?

If you are planning to purchase an R1S specifically for this feature, you should monitor the official Rivian vehicle configurator for updates on availability. As production timelines can shift, it is advisable to check the "Shop" section of the Rivian website regularly, where the company lists current inventory and upcoming build specifications.

Visit a local Rivian service center or demo event to sit in a vehicle with the new configuration once it is available, as seat comfort and aisle width can vary significantly between models. 4. Review your financing or lease options, as configuration changes can sometimes impact the final MSRP and monthly payment structure of your vehicle order.
