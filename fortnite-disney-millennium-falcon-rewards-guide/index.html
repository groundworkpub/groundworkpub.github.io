How to earn fortnite rewards through the millennium falcon ride

By Groundwork Editorial • 5 min read • Updated 2026-08-21

Key Takeaway

Learn how to link your Disney Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run experience to Fortnite to unlock exclusive in-game rewards on the Smugglers Gambit island.

  • What is the Disney and Fortnite cross-platform integration?

What is the Disney and Fortnite cross-platform integration?

An in-game integration is a digital reward system that links physical experiences at Disney theme parks to virtual items in the Fortnite ecosystem. By participating in the Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run attraction at Disneyland or Walt Disney World, players can unlock exclusive digital assets for use within Fortnite’s custom Star Wars island, Smugglers Gambit.

This partnership represents a growing trend in the entertainment industry where physical location-based entertainment (LBE) and digital gaming platforms are increasingly converged. According to Disney Games, this initiative is part of a broader strategy to create a cohesive entertainment ecosystem where stories and user engagement flow seamlessly between real-world attractions and virtual game worlds.

How do you link your Disney park experience to your Fortnite account?

To bridge your physical park visit with your Fortnite account, you must use the official Disneyland or Disney World mobile applications while on-site at the Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run attraction. Once you have initiated the mission via the app during your ride session, the system tracks your participation and flags your associated account to receive digital crates.

These crates serve as the primary unlock mechanism for content within the Smugglers Gambit island. The process requires:

1. Ensuring your official Disney park app is downloaded and updated to the latest version. 2. Logging into the app with the credentials linked to your Epic Games account. 3. Activating the "Smugglers Run" mission interface within the app while inside the Galaxy’s Edge area. 4. Completing the ride mission, where your performance as a pilot, gunner, or engineer is recorded. 5. Checking your Fortnite inventory upon returning to the game, where the rewards will be delivered automatically.

It is important to note that this integration relies on location-based triggers. You cannot claim these rewards remotely; your mobile device must be physically present within the park’s geofenced area during the attraction’s operation.

What is the Smugglers Gambit experience in Fortnite?

Smugglers Gambit is a dedicated, custom-built Star Wars island within Fortnite that serves as an extension of the Star Wars: Galaxy’s Edge narrative. Unlike standard Fortnite battle royale modes, this island functions as a persistent, high-quality immersive game that allows players to adopt the role of an Outer Rim smuggler.

Developed in-house by Disney Imagineering and Epic Games, the experience features complex gameplay loops that mirror traditional RPG mechanics. Key elements of the Smugglers Gambit experience include:

* **Co-op Missions:** Players must work together to complete objectives, emphasizing team-based strategy rather than solo competition. * **Class Selection:** You can choose specific roles—such as smuggler or gunner—that affect your gameplay style and capabilities. * **Progression Systems:** The game includes leveling up and gear acquisition, allowing for long-term character development within the Star Wars universe. * **Exploration:** The map includes newly rendered locations such as Ord Ryla and Mersa Veta Station, expanding the lore established by the theme park attractions.

By utilizing Unreal Engine 5—the same technology that powers the cockpit visuals of the Millennium Falcon ride—the developers have ensured visual and performance parity between the physical simulator and the virtual island.

Understanding the technology behind the convergence

The technological backbone of this integration is the convergence of Walt Disney Imagineering’s simulation software and Epic Games’ Unreal Engine 5. The Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run ride itself is a sophisticated flight simulator that responds to physical inputs from six riders. Every movement in the cockpit is rendered in real-time, allowing for a unique experience each time you ride.

This same engine is used to build the Smugglers Gambit island, which ensures that assets like ship models, textures, and lighting effects remain consistent across both platforms. This consistency is critical for maintaining the "story-first" approach Disney is taking with its digital gaming portfolio. By aligning the narrative of Hondo Ohnaka—the pirate character central to the Galaxy’s Edge storyline—with the missions in Fortnite, Disney is effectively extending the lifespan and depth of their park attractions beyond the physical exit gates.

Is this integration worth the effort for casual players?

Whether this integration provides significant value depends on your level of engagement with the Star Wars franchise and the Fortnite ecosystem. For dedicated players who enjoy "completionist" objectives, the ability to earn exclusive gear and unlock unique narrative paths on the Smugglers Gambit island provides a tangible incentive to participate in the physical ride.

However, the requirement to be physically present at a Disney park means this is a "high-barrier" reward system. If you are not already planning a trip to a Disney park, the cost of entry far outweighs the value of in-game Fortnite cosmetics. If you are planning a visit, the integration is a low-effort "bonus" that adds an extra layer of interactivity to your day.

To maximize your reward potential, focus on the following:

* **Optimize your role:** During the ride, prioritize your assigned tasks (piloting, shooting, or repairing) to ensure the mission completion registers correctly in the app. * **Check account synchronization:** Ensure your Epic Games account is properly linked to your Disney profile before your trip to avoid technical hurdles at the park. * **Explore the full island:** Once you access Smugglers Gambit, do not just collect the crates; engage with the co-op missions to fully utilize the gear you have unlocked.
