Google Meet’s "Take Notes for me" feature is an AI-powered tool that transcribes and summarizes audio conversations, allowing you to focus on active participation rather than manual note-taking. While originally designed for virtual video conferences, Google has expanded this functionality to support in-person meetings, using Gemini AI to capture discussions and export them directly to a Google Doc.

According to data from Google, the integration of AI note-taking is designed to reduce the cognitive load of meeting participants by automating the capture of key action items and discussion highlights. By leveraging Gemini, the system identifies speakers and context, creating a structured document that serves as an official record of your in-person collaboration.

How to use Google Meet for in-person meeting notes

To use the "Take Notes for me" feature for an in-person meeting, you must open the Google Meet app or website on your mobile device or laptop and initiate the note-taking function without starting a video call. This directs the AI to listen to the ambient room audio instead of a digital stream.

1. Launch the Google Meet app or navigate to the website on your device. 2. Locate the "Take Notes for me" option within the interface. 3. Begin the meeting and tap the record or start button to activate Gemini. 4. Place your device in a central location to ensure the microphone captures all participants clearly. 5. Use the "Pause" button if you need to go off the record during private side conversations. 6. Select "Stop" and then "Stop taking notes" once the meeting concludes. 7. Access the automatically generated Google Doc in your Google Drive to review the summary and transcript.

As reported by 9to5Google, this feature is tied to specific subscription tiers, including Google Workspace plans and Gemini AI Pro or Ultra subscriptions. If you are using a corporate account, note that your organization's administrator may need to toggle the feature on in the Google Admin console before it becomes visible to you.

Why use dedicated AI tools over traditional recording?

Traditional digital recording creates a raw audio file that requires manual review or third-party transcription services, whereas AI-integrated tools like Google Meet provide immediate, actionable summaries. By using a system that integrates with your existing workflow, you eliminate the friction of transferring files or waiting for external transcription processing.

Research suggests that real-time transcription tools improve information retention and accountability in professional settings. Unlike simple voice memos, which provide a wall of text, AI note-takers categorize information into highlights and action items, which are significantly more useful for follow-up emails and project management tracking.

What are the limitations of AI note-taking?

AI note-taking is subject to environmental factors, such as background noise, multiple people speaking simultaneously, and microphone quality. While Gemini is highly capable, it may struggle to accurately attribute speech to specific individuals if the audio quality is poor or if participants are sitting too far from the device.

Furthermore, you should consider privacy and consent before recording in-person meetings. Ensure that all participants are aware that an AI tool is listening and transcribing the conversation. In many jurisdictions, recording conversations without the explicit consent of all parties involved can violate privacy laws or company policies.

Alternatives to Google Meet for transcription

If you do not have access to Google Meet’s AI features or prefer a different interface, several alternatives offer robust transcription and summarization capabilities. According to PCMag, platforms such as Otter.ai, Rev, and GoTranscript are widely used for professional transcription needs.

* **Otter.ai:** Offers advanced speaker identification and live collaborative note-taking that works well for large group meetings. * **Apple Voice Memos:** A free, built-in option for iOS users that now includes automatic transcription, which is ideal for simple, one-on-one or small group discussions. * **Rev:** Provides a blend of automated AI transcription and optional human-verified services if high accuracy is required for sensitive or technical documents.

When choosing an alternative, prioritize tools that offer easy integration with your existing file management systems. If you rely heavily on the Google ecosystem, Google Meet remains the most efficient choice because it saves notes directly to your Google Drive, keeping your workflow centralized.
