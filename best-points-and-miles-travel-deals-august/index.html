Maximizing your travel rewards: points and miles deals for August 2026

By Groundwork Editorial • 3 min read • Updated 2026-08-21

Key Takeaway

Learn how to maximize your travel budget this August with current points and miles promotions for major airlines and hotel loyalty programs.

In This Guide

  • How do you find the best airline loyalty promotions?
  • Which hotel loyalty programs offer the most value this month?
  • How do you avoid common pitfalls when booking with points?

A points and miles travel deal is a promotional offer from airlines, hotels, or credit card issuers that allows you to earn bonus rewards or redeem existing points for lower-than-average costs. These deals are designed to increase your travel purchasing power, often by reducing the number of miles required for an award flight or providing bonus loyalty points for specific stays.

According to industry analysis, travelers who strategically leverage loyalty promotions can increase the value of their points by 20% to 50% compared to standard redemption rates (Source: NerdWallet, 2026). Whether you are looking to book a flight to Europe or secure a hotel stay, tracking these time-sensitive promotions is the most effective way to stretch your travel budget.

How do you find the best airline loyalty promotions?

Airline loyalty promotions are time-bound offers that provide either discounted award travel or accelerated point-earning opportunities. To secure the best value, you should monitor official airline newsletters, track transfer partner bonuses from major credit card issuers, and verify booking windows before committing your points to a specific itinerary.

For August 2026, Air Canada’s Aeroplan is currently offering up to 25% off select economy-class award flights (Source: Air Canada, 2026). This promotion covers a wide range of international destinations, including North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific.

1. Check the official promotion page to confirm if your desired routes are included. 2. Note the booking deadline, which is August 18, 2026, for this specific offer. 3. Ensure your travel dates fall between September 8, 2026, and June 30, 2027. 4. Verify that you have sufficient points in your Aeroplan account or a transferable credit card program (such as Chase Ultimate Rewards or American Express Membership Rewards) to complete the transfer.

Which hotel loyalty programs offer the most value this month?

Hotel loyalty promotions typically fall into two categories: bonus point incentives for stays and discounted award nights. To maximize these, you must register for the promotion through your loyalty account portal before booking your stay, as failing to register often disqualifies you from earning the bonus points.

Marriott Bonvoy is currently running a promotion offering 5,000 bonus points for stays at specific Trailborn adventure lodges, provided you use the promo code "S2449" (Source: Marriott International, 2026). Meanwhile, the World of Hyatt program is offering 3,000 bonus points per stay at select Hyatt Place and Hyatt House properties, capped at 30,000 bonus points total (Source: World of Hyatt, 2026).

To capture these deals effectively:

* Log in to your hotel loyalty account dashboard. * Navigate to the "Promotions" or "Offers" tab to register. * Confirm that the specific hotel location is participating in the promotion. * Book directly through the hotel’s website or app, as third-party travel sites (like Expedia or Booking.com) usually do not qualify for loyalty program bonuses.

How do you avoid common pitfalls when booking with points?

The primary danger when booking with points is overestimating the value of a "deal." A promotion is only valuable if you would have paid for that flight or hotel regardless of the discount. If a promotion encourages you to spend points on a category of travel that provides low "cents-per-point" value—such as redeeming miles for merchandise or gift cards—you are likely losing money.

Always calculate the cash price of a flight or room versus the points price. If the cash price is low, it is often better to pay cash and save your points for a high-value redemption, such as a long-haul business class flight or a luxury resort stay. According to The Points Guy, the average value of a point varies significantly by program, so always cross-reference your redemption against the current point valuation benchmarks (Source: The Points Guy, 2026).
