How to use the new Avios calendar search tool to find reward flights

Finding reward flight availability across the International Airlines Group (IAG) network—which includes British Airways, Iberia, and Aer Lingus—has historically been a manual, time-consuming process. Previously, travelers were forced to search day by day, making it difficult to identify flexible travel windows. The new 'Find Reward Flights' tool on the Avios website solves this by providing a calendar-based view that displays an entire month of award availability in a single interface.

According to IAG, this tool allows loyalty program members to search for nonstop award seats across its three primary carriers without the need for third-party scrapers or manual daily checks. By centralizing this data, the tool simplifies the process of securing high-value redemptions, particularly in premium cabins where availability is often limited and highly competitive.

How to access and use the calendar search

To utilize the tool, you must have a registered Avios loyalty account, which is free to open. Once logged in, navigate to the reward flight search section. The interface is designed for simplicity: you enter your origin and destination airports, the number of passengers, and the month you intend to travel.

Once you submit your search criteria, the tool generates a monthly calendar. Dates with available award seats are marked with circles, indicating the level of availability. A half-filled circle typically indicates limited inventory, while a full circle suggests a higher probability of securing multiple seats. Hovering over a specific date provides a snapshot of available cabins and the starting cost in Avios. Clicking on a date allows you to view specific flight times and proceed toward the booking process.

Maximizing value with Iberia and Aer Lingus

While British Airways is the most prominent carrier in the IAG portfolio, the new tool is arguably most valuable for identifying sweet spots on Iberia and Aer Lingus. Iberia, in particular, is frequently cited for offering lower taxes and surcharges on transatlantic flights compared to British Airways, which often imposes significant carrier-imposed surcharges on long-haul routes.

By using the calendar view, you can quickly scan for Iberia's off-peak pricing, which can significantly reduce the number of Avios required for business class seats to Madrid. Because the tool displays an entire month at once, you can easily compare costs across different dates to find the absolute minimum mileage requirement for your trip. This capability is essential for travelers who have flexibility in their schedule and want to extract the maximum cent-per-point value from their loyalty balances.

Understanding the current limitations

While the tool is a significant upgrade, it is important to understand its current functional boundaries. First, the tool is strictly limited to nonstop flights. If you are looking for an itinerary that requires a connection—for example, flying from a secondary city to a hub and then on to your final destination—the tool will not display these options. IAG has indicated that connecting flight functionality is on the development roadmap, but as of now, you must search for each leg of a journey individually.

Second, the tool does not display the final cash component of the ticket. Taxes, fees, and surcharges can vary significantly between carriers. Even if you find an available seat, the out-of-pocket cost for that seat on a British Airways flight may be substantially higher than on an Aer Lingus flight due to different fuel surcharge structures. You will need to click through to the final checkout page on the respective airline’s website to see the exact cash requirement before committing.

Finally, the tool is a discovery engine, not a booking engine. Once you identify a flight that meets your needs, the tool redirects you to the specific airline’s portal (British Airways Executive Club, Iberia Plus, or Aer Lingus AerClub) to complete the transaction. This transition can occasionally result in technical friction if your Avios are not already linked or consolidated across these programs.

Strategic next steps for your next booking

To make the most of this tool, follow these steps:

1. Consolidate your Avios: Ensure your Avios accounts across British Airways, Iberia, and Aer Lingus are linked. This allows you to move points between programs instantly, which is vital if you find a better price on one carrier versus another. 2. Prioritize flexibility: If your goal is to travel in business class, use the calendar view to look at an entire month rather than a specific date. If you see no availability, check the following month immediately. 3. Verify total costs: Before transferring points or committing to a flight, always check the total taxes and fees on the airline's own booking page. Do not assume the Avios cost is the only expense. 4. Monitor for off-peak dates: Use the calendar to specifically hunt for off-peak pricing windows, which can save you thousands of Avios on long-haul routes.

By treating this tool as a high-level planning instrument rather than a final checkout solution, you can avoid the frustration of manual searching and secure more rewarding travel experiences.
