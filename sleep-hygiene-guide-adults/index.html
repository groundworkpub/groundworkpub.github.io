Why sleep quality declines after 35

Sleep architecture changes with age. Deep sleep decreases, and night waking becomes more common. But most of the decline is manageable with deliberate routines.

The core sleep hygiene pillars

  • Consistent wake time every day, including weekends
  • Bright light within 30 minutes of waking
  • No caffeine after 2pm
  • A cool, dark bedroom around 65°F

A simple evening wind-down

Start 60 minutes before bed: dim lights, stop work, avoid screens, and keep the room cool. Consistency beats intensity.
