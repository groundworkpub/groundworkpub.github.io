How to disable Find My before selling or trading your iPhone

By Groundwork Editorial • 3 min read • Updated 2026-08-21

Key Takeaway

Disabling Find My is essential before selling or gifting your iPhone. Learn how to turn off Activation Lock and perform a factory reset to protect your data.

In This Guide

  • Why you must disable Find My before transferring your iPhone
  • How to disable Find My on your iPhone
  • How to disable Stolen Device Protection
  • Why a factory reset is the final essential step

Why you must disable Find My before transferring your iPhone

Find My is a security feature that links your iPhone to your Apple ID, effectively locking the device to your account. This mechanism, known as Activation Lock, is designed to prevent unauthorized users from accessing your phone if it is lost or stolen. When you sell, trade, or gift your iPhone, you must disable this feature; otherwise, the new owner will be unable to set up the device, as it will remain tethered to your credentials. According to Apple's official support documentation, removing this link is a mandatory step in the device preparation process (Apple Support, 2024).

How to disable Find My on your iPhone

To disable Find My, you must navigate through your Apple ID settings menu while the device is in your possession. This process verifies that you are the authorized owner and removes the Activation Lock from Apple’s servers. Follow these steps to turn off the service:

1. Open the **Settings** app on your iPhone. 2. Tap your name at the very top of the menu to access your Apple ID settings. 3. Tap **Find My**. 4. Select **Find My iPhone**. 5. Toggle the **Find My iPhone** switch to the off position. 6. Enter your Apple ID password when prompted to confirm the change.

Once completed, the device is no longer tracked or locked to your account, allowing the next user to associate the hardware with their own Apple ID.

How to disable Stolen Device Protection

If you have enabled Stolen Device Protection, you may find that the Find My toggle is greyed out or inaccessible. Apple introduced this security feature to add a layer of biometric verification for sensitive account changes when your device is away from familiar locations like your home or work. You must disable this feature before you can turn off Find My. Follow these steps:

1. Open the **Settings** app. 2. Scroll down and tap **Face ID & Passcode** (or **Touch ID & Passcode**). 3. Enter your device passcode when prompted. 4. Locate **Stolen Device Protection** in the list. 5. Toggle the switch to **Off**.

After disabling this setting, you can return to the Find My menu and successfully toggle it off. Note that if you are in a "familiar location," the change may be immediate; if you are in an unfamiliar location, Apple may enforce a security delay before the setting can be modified (Apple Support, 2024).

Why a factory reset is the final essential step

Disabling Find My is only the first part of preparing your device for a new user. You must also perform a full factory reset to ensure all personal data, including photos, messages, and saved passwords, are permanently removed from the device’s storage. Simply deleting files manually is insufficient, as data remnants can often be recovered by sophisticated software. A factory reset wipes the encryption keys used to secure your data, rendering the remaining information unreadable.

To perform a factory reset, go to **Settings > General > Transfer or Reset iPhone > Erase All Content and Settings**. This process will restart the phone and present the initial "Hello" setup screen, confirming that the device is wiped and ready for a new owner. Always ensure you have a current iCloud or computer backup before beginning this process, as all data will be permanently erased during the reset.
