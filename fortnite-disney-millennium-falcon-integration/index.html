How to earn Fortnite rewards through the Millennium Falcon ride

A Disney-Fortnite integration is an interactive experience that allows visitors of the Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run ride to unlock digital rewards for their Fortnite accounts. By using official Disney park applications while participating in the attraction, you can trigger in-game assets that transfer to the custom Star Wars island, Smugglers Gambit, within the Fortnite ecosystem.

According to Disney and Epic Games, this initiative represents a strategic shift toward a converged entertainment model, where physical theme park experiences directly influence digital gaming progression (CNET, 2026). This convergence aims to bridge the gap between real-world location-based entertainment and virtual, persistent game worlds.

How do you link your Disney and Fortnite accounts for rewards? To earn rewards, you must use the official Disneyland or Disney World mobile application while physically present at the Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run attraction. Once you initiate the ride experience, you can accept a specific mission through the app that tracks your participation and unlocks digital crates for your Fortnite account. These crates contain assets that are usable within the Smugglers Gambit island.

It is essential to ensure that your Epic Games account is correctly linked to your Disney digital profile before visiting the park. Without an active connection, the system cannot verify your ride participation or transmit the digital assets to your inventory. Based on the technical framework provided by Epic Games, the integration utilizes the location-based data from the Disney app to validate your presence on the ride.

What is the Smugglers Gambit experience in Fortnite? Smugglers Gambit is a custom-built, immersive game island within Fortnite that serves as an extension of the Star Wars galaxy established in Disney Parks. Developed by Disney Games, Lucasfilm, and Walt Disney Imagineering, the island allows players to assume the role of an Outer Rim smuggler. The gameplay focuses on cooperative missions, character leveling, and gear acquisition, mirroring the narrative themes found at Galaxy’s Edge.

Within this environment, you can explore new locations such as Ord Ryla and Mersa Veta Station. The gameplay loop involves: 1. Engaging in co-op missions with other players. 2. Selecting specific character classes to suit your playstyle. 3. Battling against diverse threats, including pirate factions, Imperial remnants, and planetary wildlife. 4. Earning and upgrading gear to increase your effectiveness in later stages.

This island effectively functions as a digital expansion of the Hondo Ohnaka narrative, continuing the story of the pirate-turned-Resistance ally first introduced in the Star Wars: The Clone Wars television series.

Understanding the technology behind the ride and the game The Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run ride and the Smugglers Gambit game share a common technical foundation: Unreal Engine 5. This engine powers the high-fidelity visuals found in the cockpit simulator and the in-game world of Fortnite, ensuring visual consistency between the physical ride experience and the virtual game island.

Walt Disney Imagineering collaborated with Industrial Light & Magic to build the cockpit simulation, which relies on sophisticated motion-tracking and real-time rendering. By leveraging the same engine for both the ride's cockpit and the Fortnite island, Disney has created a seamless transition for users who move between the physical attraction and the digital game. This technical parity is a key component of Disney’s long-term strategy to unify their diverse intellectual properties under a single, interoperable digital framework.

How to maximize your rewards from the experience To get the most value from this integration, you should approach the ride with a strategy geared toward collection. Because the rewards are tied to specific crate drops during the mission, your performance within the ride matters. Since the ride requires six participants to function—two pilots, two gunners, and two engineers—coordination with your fellow riders can directly impact the quality of the mission completion.

Follow these steps to optimize your experience: 1. Verify your mobile app permissions: Ensure your Disneyland or Disney World app has location services enabled so it can detect your presence on the ride. 2. Coordinate roles: If you are riding with a group, ensure everyone understands their role (pilot, gunner, or engineer) to maximize the team's score during the flight. 3. Check your inventory: After completing the ride, sync your account in the Fortnite interface to confirm that the crates have been credited to your account. 4. Explore Smugglers Gambit: Use the newly acquired gear immediately to gain an advantage in the early levels of the Smugglers Gambit island.

By integrating these two distinct entertainment mediums, Disney and Epic Games have created a "phygital" (physical and digital) loop that incentivizes repeat visits to the parks and sustained engagement with the Fortnite platform. This model is likely to serve as a blueprint for future collaborations across Disney’s expansive portfolio of film and television franchises.
