How to use your Amex Resy credit at Tock restaurants

An Amex Resy credit is a recurring statement benefit provided to select American Express cardholders that reimburses eligible dining purchases made at participating restaurants booked through the Resy platform. Following the acquisition and integration of Tock into the Resy ecosystem, American Express has expanded this benefit to include a broader range of high-end dining venues, wineries, and culinary experiences. Understanding the timeline and eligibility criteria is essential for maximizing this financial perk.

As of August 2024, Resy and Tock have officially integrated, bringing over 25,000 restaurants and venues under a unified digital umbrella. According to reporting from The Points Guy, this merger significantly increases the inventory of bookable experiences, specifically targeting the luxury dining and tasting menu sector (The Points Guy, 2024). While the integration is live, the eligibility for statement credits is subject to a phased rollout.

Understanding the phased credit rollout

Not every restaurant found on the combined Resy platform qualifies for your statement credit immediately. When you browse the platform, you will notice specific venues tagged with an eligibility badge. American Express has clarified that this badge is used to distinguish between legacy Resy restaurants and the newly added Tock venues that may not yet meet the criteria for the credit (American Express, 2024).

Starting September 15, 2024, hundreds of U.S.-based Tock venues transitioned into the eligible pool for the Resy statement credit. This expansion is not static; American Express has indicated that additional venues will be added on a rolling basis throughout the remainder of the year. If a restaurant you frequent is not currently eligible, it may be added to the program in future updates. Always check the Resy app or website for the specific credit-eligible badge before finalizing your booking or payment.

How to verify your restaurant eligibility

To ensure your dining purchase triggers the statement credit, you must verify the status of the restaurant within the Resy interface. Because the integration is ongoing, relying on past knowledge of a restaurant’s affiliation is insufficient. Follow these steps to confirm eligibility before you dine:

1. Open the Resy app or visit the official Resy website. 2. Search for the specific restaurant, winery, or dining experience you intend to visit. 3. Look for the "Amex Resy Credit" badge or indicator on the restaurant’s profile page. If this badge is absent, the transaction will not trigger the statement credit. 4. Ensure you are using the specific American Express card associated with the Resy benefit to pay for your meal.

American Express notes that the badge system does not remove existing restaurants from eligibility; it serves only to provide clarity on the new, expanded network (The Points Guy, 2024). If you are uncertain about a venue, check the "Amex Benefits" section within your Resy account profile, which provides updated lists of participating merchants in your area.

Maximizing your quarterly and monthly credits

Different American Express cards carry varying credit structures, ranging from monthly allowances to quarterly caps. For example, The Platinum Card® from American Express offers up to $100 in statement credits per quarter at eligible U.S. Resy restaurants. Because these benefits often reset on a strict calendar schedule—such as October 1 for quarterly benefits—timing your dining reservations around the September 15 expansion is a strategic way to avoid losing unused funds.

If you have a quarterly credit, you must settle your bill at an eligible venue before the quarter ends. Because the credit is applied as a statement statement, the transaction must post to your account before the cutoff date to count toward that period's allotment. If you dine on the final days of the quarter, ensure the restaurant processes the charge promptly to avoid a delay that pushes the credit into the next cycle.

Practical steps for cardholders

To effectively leverage this benefit, you must be proactive about tracking your spending and verifying your payment method. Follow this workflow to ensure you never miss a credit:

1. Enroll your card: Ensure your eligible American Express card is linked to your Resy account. This is a one-time requirement but is necessary for the system to track your eligibility automatically. 2. Monitor the expiration: If you hold a card with a quarterly benefit, set a reminder for the last week of each quarter (March, June, September, and December) to ensure you have utilized the full amount. 3. Use the right card: If you hold multiple Amex cards, only the card specifically linked to the Resy benefit will trigger the reimbursement. Using a different card—even if it is also an American Express product—will result in no credit. 4. Check the receipt: After dining, verify that the merchant name on your Amex transaction matches the name of the eligible restaurant listed on Resy. If you are paying for a prepaid experience (like a ticketed tasting menu), ensure the transaction is processed directly by the restaurant rather than a third-party ticketing platform, as this can sometimes impact credit eligibility.

By following these steps, you can bridge the gap between the traditional Resy network and the newly integrated Tock venues, ensuring your dining budget works as hard as possible. While the expansion adds convenience, the responsibility remains on the cardholder to verify eligibility through the official badge system before payment.
