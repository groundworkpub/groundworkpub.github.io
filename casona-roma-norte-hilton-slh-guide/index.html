How to book Casona Roma Norte using hotel loyalty points

By Groundwork Editorial • 3 min read • Updated 2026-08-21

Key Takeaway

Learn how to book the Michelin-recognized Casona Roma Norte in Mexico City using Hilton Honors or World of Hyatt points through their new loyalty partnerships.

In This Guide

  • How does the Hilton and SLH partnership work?
  • What should you know before booking your stay?
  • Which loyalty programs can you use at this property?
  • What amenities does Casona Roma Norte offer?

Casona Roma Norte is a boutique hotel in Mexico City that recently joined the Small Luxury Hotels of the World (SLH) collection, allowing Hilton Honors members to book stays using points. A hotel partnership is a formal agreement between independent properties and major loyalty programs, enabling guests to earn or redeem points at boutique locations (The Points Guy, 2024).

As of recent industry updates, Casona Roma Norte holds a 1-Michelin Key distinction, a rating system introduced by the Michelin Guide to recognize exceptional boutique hotels that offer unique guest experiences and high-quality service (Michelin Guide, 2024). The property is housed in a restored 1920s Belle Epoque mansion, featuring 32 suites and four distinct dining concepts, including the rooftop lounge Somma and the fine-dining restaurant Terra.

How does the Hilton and SLH partnership work?

The Hilton and SLH partnership allows Hilton Honors members to earn and redeem points at participating SLH properties globally. By integrating these independent hotels into the Hilton booking ecosystem, members can use their points to secure stays at unique, non-chain properties while still enjoying the reliability of the Hilton booking platform (Hilton, 2024).

To book Casona Roma Norte using Hilton Honors points, follow these steps: 1. Log in to your Hilton Honors account on the official Hilton website or mobile app. 2. Enter "Mexico City" as your destination and select your travel dates. 3. Check the box labeled "Use Points & Money" or "Use Hilton Honors Points" before initiating your search. 4. Browse the results to see if Casona Roma Norte is available for your selected dates. 5. Select your preferred room type and proceed to checkout to finalize your reservation using your point balance.

What should you know before booking your stay?

Casona Roma Norte maintains specific policies regarding guest demographics and amenities that differ from standard large-scale hotels. The property is strictly for guests aged 12 and older, meaning it is not suitable for families traveling with younger children. Additionally, while the hotel is pet-friendly, you should always confirm specific weight limits or pet fees directly with the property before finalizing your booking to avoid unexpected charges upon arrival.

Which loyalty programs can you use at this property?

Casona Roma Norte is uniquely positioned within two major loyalty ecosystems: the Hilton Honors program through its SLH membership and the World of Hyatt program through its affiliation with Mr & Mrs Smith. This dual-membership structure provides travelers with significant flexibility when choosing how to fund their stay (Mr & Mrs Smith, 2024).

If you are choosing between programs, consider the following factors: * **Point valuation:** Compare the cash rate of the room against the required point redemption rate for both Hilton and Hyatt to determine which provides higher value per point. * **Transfer partners:** Determine if you have flexible credit card points (such as Chase Ultimate Rewards or American Express Membership Rewards) that can be transferred to either Hilton or Hyatt to reach the redemption threshold faster. * **Elite benefits:** Check if your existing status with either Hilton or Hyatt provides on-site perks, such as complimentary breakfast or late checkout, which may be honored at the property.

What amenities does Casona Roma Norte offer?

The property features four distinct dining concepts designed to showcase local gastronomy, ranging from casual tea rooms to high-end culinary experiences.

* **La Macaria Matcha & Tea Room:** Focuses on artisanal pastries and specialty teas. * **Somma Rooftop:** Serves as a social club and wine bar with views of the city. * **Terra:** Offers a fine-dining menu with curated beverage pairings. * **Tacos Romita:** Provides a localized taqueria experience highlighting regional flavors.

Given the hotel's location in the Roma Norte neighborhood, guests are situated within walking distance of several cultural landmarks and parks, making it a strategic home base for exploring Mexico City's historic architecture and vibrant street life.
