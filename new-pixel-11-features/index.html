four key features of the new pixel 11 devices

By Groundwork Editorial • 4 min read • Updated 2026-08-21

Key Takeaway

the new pixel 11 series comes with four key features: improved cameras, upgraded charging, the tensor g6 chip, and a minimum of 256gb storage

In This Guide

  • what are the key features of the new pixel 11 devices? The new Pixel 11 series comes with four key features that are available across all devices, including the Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, and Pixel 11 Pro Fold. These features include improved cameras, upgraded charging, the Tensor G6 chip, and a minimum of 256GB storage.
  • what camera upgrades can you expect from the pixel 11 series? Every Pixel 11 phone has improved cameras, with the Pixel 11 featuring a 48MP main camera that is 56% more sensitive to light, according to Google. The Pixel 11 Pro and Pixel 11 Pro XL also get brand new sensors on the main cameras, while the telephoto camera gets bumped up to 48MP. Google says its new image sensor is 30% more light-sensitive than before, as reported by Lifehacker. The Pros also support Portrait Mode at 5x zoom, and 'Pro Zoom' at 120x. Additionally, Night Sight is now 4.5x faster than it was on Pixel 10 Pro.
  • how has charging been upgraded in the pixel 11 series? No matter which Pixel 11 device you choose, Google says you get upgraded charging over last year's Pixel 10. The Pixel 11 gets the fewest upgrades here, with Google saying it has the 'fastest charging yet' using PixelSnap built-in. All three 'Pro' Pixel 11 devices now support Qi 2.2, a faster wireless charging standard. If you use a 25W charger, you should notice quicker speeds when wirelessly charging. The Pixel 11 Pro XL might be the phone to get if charging speeds are your thing, with Google saying you can get 15 hours of use with 15 minutes of charging using a 45W USB-C charger.
  • what are the benefits of the tensor g6 chip in the pixel 11 series? All Pixel 11 devices come with the Tensor G6, Google's latest chip. The company says Tensor G6 is the 'fastest, most powerful' Google chip yet, and features a CPU that supports 25% faster web browsing and 15% faster app launches. The company claims the new chip can power on-device AI tasks 3.5x faster than Tensor G5, while at the same time being 3.5x more efficient than before, as stated by Google.
  • what storage options are available for the pixel 11 series? Every Pixel 11 device now starts at 256GB, saying goodbye to 128GB of storage. This means that regardless of which device you choose, you'll have plenty of storage space for your apps, photos, and other data.
