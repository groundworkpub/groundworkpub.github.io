👉 Read the Full Study on Groundwork: Is the Soundcore Boom 2 Plus worth the investment?

A portable speaker is a wireless audio device designed for high-volume output in outdoor or large indoor settings, and the Soundcore Boom 2 Plus is a high-wattage model currently positioned as a mid-range value option. With a current market price of approximately $170, it competes with premium audio brands by offering high-output drivers and ruggedized features.

According to data from PCMag, the device delivers a significant low-frequency response that separates it from standard, smaller Bluetooth speakers. Whether you are hosting a pool party or camping, the hardware is designed to maintain sound fidelity at higher volume levels.

What makes the Soundcore Boom 2 Plus a high-output speaker?

The Soundcore Boom 2 Plus produces audio through a 140W system featuring two 50W woofers and two 20W tweeters. This configuration is specifically engineered for wide-area sound dispersion, making it suitable for open-air environments where smaller speakers would fail to project clearly.

Industry testing confirms that the internal digital signal processing (DSP), specifically the BassUp 2.0 technology, enhances the lower end of the frequency spectrum. While this mode is active by default, users can toggle it via the Soundcore companion app to adjust the sound signature based on the acoustics of their current environment.

Can you use this speaker near water?

The Soundcore Boom 2 Plus carries an IPX7 ingress protection rating, which means the device is designed to withstand immersion in up to one meter of water for a duration of 30 minutes. Critically, the chassis is engineered to float, preventing the unit from sinking to the bottom of a pool or lake if dropped.

While the IPX7 rating provides peace of mind for poolside use, always ensure the charging port cover is securely sealed before exposing the unit to moisture. Exposure to salt water should be followed by a gentle rinse with fresh water to prevent mineral buildup and corrosion on the charging contacts.

How does the battery life perform under load?

Under laboratory-tested conditions, the Soundcore Boom 2 Plus provides up to 20 hours of playback time. However, this figure is based on specific usage parameters, typically 50% volume and with the BassUp feature disabled.

If you consistently run the speaker at maximum volume with the LED lighting effects enabled and BassUp active, you should anticipate a significant reduction in total runtime. For extended outdoor trips, consider these steps to maximize battery efficiency: 1. Keep the volume at a moderate level for ambient listening. 2. Disable the integrated LED light show through the Soundcore app. 3. Turn off BassUp mode when playing acoustic or vocal-heavy tracks. 4. Ensure the speaker is fully charged before leaving home.

Is the weight and design manageable for travel?

Weighing 8.4 pounds, the speaker is substantial, which is a trade-off for its high-wattage hardware and rugged casing. It is not designed for ultralight backpacking, but it is portable enough for car camping or short walks to a beach spot.

The inclusion of a removable, adjustable strap and a reinforced handle improves ergonomics, allowing you to carry it comfortably over the shoulder. The chassis is constructed primarily of high-impact plastic, which provides a balance between weight reduction and structural durability against drops or impacts.

How to get the most out of your portable speaker

To ensure your investment lasts, use the Soundcore app to perform firmware updates. Manufacturers often release updates that improve Bluetooth connectivity stability and refine the sound profile. Additionally, avoid leaving the lithium-ion battery in extreme temperatures, such as a hot car, as this can degrade battery capacity over time.
